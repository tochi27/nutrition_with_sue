Two more simple habits from *Eat Smart, Live Well*: what you eat first, and how you eat it.

## Habit 02: Eat your fruits and vegetables first

This small shift can make a surprisingly big impact. Starting your meal with fruits or vegetables helps your body receive fibre and water early in the meal, which promotes fullness and slows digestion.

Fibre-rich foods help regulate blood sugar levels and support satiety — meaning you're less likely to overeat later in the meal.

### Try it like this

- Start dinner with a small salad
- Eat your vegetables before your rice, pasta, or protein
- Have a piece of fruit before reaching for dessert

Research in appetite regulation suggests that beginning meals with low-energy, high-fibre foods can naturally reduce total calorie intake without requiring strict dieting. It's a simple strategy that works with your body's natural hunger signals.

> Sustainable nutrition is not about perfection — it's about building healthy habits that work for your life.

## Habit 03: Eat without distractions (practise mindful eating)

Many of us eat while scrolling on our phones, watching television, or working at our desks. While it may seem harmless, distracted eating often leads to mindless overeating.

When we eat while distracted, the brain does not fully register the meal. As a result, we may eat more than our body actually needs. Mindful eating encourages you to pay attention to the experience of eating, helping you recognise hunger and fullness signals more clearly.

### Mindful eating practices

- Put your phone away during meals
- Turn off the TV
- Sit down and focus on your food
- Notice the taste, texture, and aroma of each bite

When you eat with awareness, you often feel more satisfied with less food.
