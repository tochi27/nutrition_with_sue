In today's world of trending diets and conflicting nutrition advice, eating well can feel unnecessarily complicated. But sustainable health is built on simple daily habits — small, consistent changes that make a powerful difference in weight, energy, and overall wellbeing.

As a nutritionist, I often remind my clients that many people believe they need strict meal plans or drastic changes to lose weight or maintain a healthy one. Here are five simple, evidence-based habits that can transform the way you eat. We start with the first.

## Habit 01: Always include fruits and vegetables with your meals

A balanced meal is one that nourishes the body with essential nutrients while also helping you feel satisfied. One of the easiest ways to achieve this is by always including fruits or vegetables alongside your meals.

Fruits and vegetables are packed with nutrients that support digestion, heart health, and immune function — while helping you feel full with fewer calories:

- Dietary fibre
- Vitamins and minerals
- Antioxidants
- Natural water content

Research consistently shows that diets rich in fruits and vegetables are associated with better weight management and reduced risk of chronic diseases. A helpful guideline: **fill half your plate with vegetables or fruits whenever possible.**

### Simple ways to do this

- Add berries, bananas, or apples to breakfast
- Include a side salad or steamed vegetables with lunch
- Add roasted or sautéed vegetables to dinner
- Choose fruit as a snack instead of processed options

Rather than focusing on restricting foods, start by adding more nourishing foods to your plate.
