The last two habits from *Eat Smart, Live Well* are about timing: slowing down, and reaching for water before food.

## Habit 04: Eat slowly

Your body needs time to communicate fullness to your brain. In fact, it typically takes about 20 minutes for satiety signals to develop. When meals are eaten too quickly, it's easy to consume more food before the body has time to signal that it's full.

Eating slowly allows your digestive system and appetite hormones to function properly.

### Practical ways to slow down

- Take smaller bites
- Chew your food thoroughly
- Put your fork down between bites
- Pause halfway through the meal to check if you're still hungry

People who eat more slowly often find that they naturally eat less while feeling equally satisfied.

## Habit 05: Drink water first

One of the simplest habits for supporting healthy eating is drinking water before you eat. Sometimes the body confuses thirst with hunger — drinking water before meals can help ensure that what you're feeling is true hunger rather than dehydration.

Hydration also supports digestion, metabolism, and overall health. Some studies suggest that drinking water before meals may help reduce calorie intake, making it a simple yet powerful strategy for weight management.

### Try this simple habit

- Drink a glass of water 10–15 minutes before meals
- Start your morning with water before breakfast
- Keep a water bottle nearby throughout the day

## The bottom line

Healthy eating does not need to be complicated or restrictive. In fact, the most effective strategies are often the simplest. By practising these five habits consistently, you can:

- Improve your relationship with food
- Support a healthy weight
- Nourish your body with essential nutrients
- Reduce mindless overeating

Start with one habit at a time and allow it to become part of your daily routine. Over time, these small changes can lead to lasting improvements in both your health and wellbeing.

Remember: sustainable nutrition is not about perfection — it's about building healthy habits that work for your life.
