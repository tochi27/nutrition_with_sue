Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how Susan Emuze, trading as Nutrition with Sue ("I", "me"), collects and uses your personal information when you use this website or get in touch. I am the data controller for this information.

What I collect

  • Newsletter sign-ups: your first name and email address.
  • Bookings (through Calendly): your name, email address, the appointment you choose, and your answers to any booking questions, which may include information about your health and goals.
  • Contact requests: your name, email address, the service you're interested in, and anything you choose to tell me about your health and goals.

Information about your health is "special category" data under UK GDPR. I only collect it with your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the consent box on the contact form, or by choosing to share it when you book.

How I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange consultations (lawful basis: steps prior to entering a contract, and your explicit consent for health information).
  • To send you the newsletter, if you've subscribed (lawful basis: consent). You can unsubscribe at any time.

I will never sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing.

Who else processes it

  • Calendly provides the booking calendar and stores the details you enter when you book an appointment. Calendly is based in the United States.
  • EmailJS receives the details you submit through the contact and newsletter forms and sends them to my email inbox.
  • Netlify, Inc. hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address. Netlify is based in the United States.
  • Google Fonts supplies the website's fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page loads.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to a consultation are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as professional guidance requires. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your information, to restrict or object to how it's used, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, email emuze.susan123@yahoo.com.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website itself does not set any cookies or use analytics tracking. The Calendly booking calendar in the "Book a Consultation" section may set its own cookies, which are needed for booking to work. Calendly shows its own cookie notice inside the calendar, and you can read more in Calendly's privacy notice.
