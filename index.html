From the Blog

Small habits, big difference

🥦

Habit 01

Always Include Fruit & Veg

Half your plate, every meal — for fibre, vitamins and lasting fullness.

Berries at breakfast · Side salad at lunch · Roasted veg at dinner

🍽️

Habits 02 & 03

Veg First, Eat Mindfully

Starting with vegetables steadies blood sugar and helps you feel full.

Veg before rice · Phone down · Savour each bite

💧

Habits 04 & 05

Slow Down, Water First

Your brain takes about 20 minutes to register fullness — give it time.

Chew well · Pause mid-meal · Water 10–15 mins before
